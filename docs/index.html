Hindsight · built with Gemini 4 Argon

Federated Robot Learning: Enabled by Argon

Robots that learn from their own failures, with Argon finding, rebuilding, retraining and approving every fix.

1

Robot fleets collect thousands of hours of data.

Every robot records everything: camera, motion sensors, commands. We used real public footage from FrodoBots robots, indoors and outdoors.

2

Engineers don't have time to review the failures.

Failures are rare and buried. Sensor alarms are mostly noise: video glitches, speed bumps, ramps.

Argon reviews them and keeps the failures that matter.

4

Argon builds a digital twin of each failure, then makes it harder.

The twin has to reproduce the real crash before it is trusted. Argon then generates 40 harder what-if variants, and can make the twin look like the real camera.

Real robot cameraArgon's 3D twinTwin + Gemini Omni
5

Argon writes the reward function by watching good and bad rides.

It reads two smooth rides and four real crashes and turns them into reward code.

Rewards

  • steady forward progress
  • wide clearance around furniture, pets and people
  • smooth speed and steering

Penalizes

  • tipping or falling over
  • collisions and near-misses
  • stalling, ramming, twitchy driving
6

A reinforcement learning loop builds a better policy.

The robot retrains on the twin and its variants.

7

Argon checks for cheating and fixes its own reward.

RL finds shortcuts. Argon watches the result, names the hack, rewrites the reward and retrains, on its own in a Google-hosted sandbox.

8

Before shipping, Argon checks the fix against every other site.

Robots share only the small policy update, not their video. Argon tests each update on every site's scenarios and blocks anything that causes a regression elsewhere.
